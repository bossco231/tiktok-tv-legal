TV Viewer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains how TV Viewer (the “App”), an independently developed third-party application for viewing TikTok videos on compatible devices, handles information.

1. Information the App requests

The App is designed to request only information needed for its viewing and sign-in features. If you choose to sign in, TikTok may provide authorization information and basic account information permitted by the permissions you approve. The App does not ask you to enter or disclose your TikTok password to the App developer.

2. How information is used

Information made available through an authorization flow is used to support the App's requested features. Search terms and playback requests are used to find and play the content you request.

3. Storage and sharing

Some settings, search history, cached content, or authorization data may be stored locally on your device to support operation. Requests needed to search for, authorize, or play TikTok content may be sent to TikTok or other service providers involved in delivery of that content. Those third parties handle information under their own privacy policies.

4. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored App data can generally be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it; doing so may sign you out or remove local settings. Authorization can also be revoked through TikTok's account settings. Data held by TikTok or other third parties must be managed with those services.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of connected services.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps in the App's design to limit information handling, but no device, network, or software system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the App's features change. For privacy questions, contact the App developer through the support channel where you obtained the App.
